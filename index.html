Hello there!

I'm Anastasia, and I'm a PhD candidate in the Responsible AI Group at Utrecht University.
My supervisors are Dr. Pınar Yolum and Dr. Pradeep Murukannaiah

Currently, my research project is on responsible autonomy for Hybrid Intelligence where I'm focusing on developing a dynamic consent model for human-AI collaborative systems. What this means is that I'm teaching AI how consent works between humans so it can better protect our data and deliberate on who can access what, when!
